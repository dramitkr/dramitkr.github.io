Voting help

Questions about voting

Quick answers so your ballot doesn’t go uncast. Official election information comes only from the IEEE India Council and IEEE vTools Voting.

When is the voting?
Voting for IEEE India Council Chair-Elect 2027 runs from 19 November to 8 December 2026. Check the ballot email for the exact closing time.
How do I vote?
Go to voting.vtools.ieee.org, sign in with your IEEE account, open the IEEE India Council Chair-Elect 2027 ballot, make your choice, and click Complete Ballot. You’ll also receive a ballot email from IEEE vTools Voting with a direct link. See the step-by-step guide.
I can’t find my ballot. What should I do?
  1. You don’t need the email — sign in directly at voting.vtools.ieee.org; your ballot appears under Ballots.
  2. Check your spam, junk and promotions folders.
  3. Search your inbox for “vTools” or “IEEE India Council”.
  4. Make sure the email address in your IEEE account is current — sign in at ieee.org and check your account settings.
  5. If it still hasn’t arrived, contact the IEEE India Council through its official website, ieeeindiacouncil.org.
I voted but I’m not sure it counted.
After choosing, you must click Complete Ballot on the Confirm Vote page. If you see Vote Completed, your vote is recorded. If not, sign in again at voting.vtools.ieee.org and finish the ballot.
Who is eligible to vote?
Eligibility is set by the IEEE India Council election rules and depends on your membership grade and Section. If you are eligible, the ballot email is sent to you automatically — you don’t need to register. For questions about eligibility, please contact the IEEE India Council.
Is my vote private?
You vote directly on IEEE’s own platform. Candidates, including me, do not see how any individual member voted.
Does this website collect my details or ask for my IEEE login?
No. This site has no forms, sets no cookies and never asks for your IEEE credentials. Only enter your IEEE login on official IEEE pages.
How can I help?
Vote as soon as your ballot arrives, and remind colleagues in your Section — personally, one to one. Please don’t use IEEE mailing lists, vTools/e-Notice, or official IEEE Section, Chapter or Council pages and groups to promote a candidate. Share this page or download a poster.

About

Forensic scientist, entrepreneur, engineer — and IEEE volunteer.

I founded BioAxis DNA Research Centre in Hyderabad with a US collaborator, as India’s first private DNA testing company. Our laboratory has supported more than 750 criminal and non-criminal cases, I have trained over 1,000 crime investigation officers, and since 2012 our group has worked on genetic predisposition studies that help cancer patients.

In IEEE I started where most volunteers do — as Hyderabad Section webmaster and vTools coordinator — and went on to serve as Secretary, Treasurer, Vice Chair and Chair before taking on India Council, Region 10 and IEEE-wide roles. Along the way I have run conferences, conference leadership programmes, entrepreneurship development workshops, and innovation and internship trainings.

As Chair-Elect, I want that experience working for every member in every Section across India.
