If elected — priorities & objectives

An India Council that works for every Section — not only the largest.

Industry participation

  • An India Council industry forum, with anchor employers engaged in every Section
  • Internships, projects and continuing education routed to student and young professional members

Benefits members actually feel

  • An annual skills and certification calendar reaching every Section, not only the largest
  • Awards and recognition that find volunteers across all fourteen Sections

India seen globally

  • Indian volunteers nominated, prepared and placed on Region 10 and IEEE-level committees
  • India-hosted conferences positioned as global events, not local ones

Stronger Sections and chapters

  • Chapters formed where there are none, dormant ones revived, new chairs supported through year one
  • Conference organisers trained, with technical and organisational integrity non-negotiable

About

An entrepreneur who knows what volunteers need.

Running a pioneering company teaches you that good intentions need structure behind them. That is the approach I have brought to IEEE — whether founding the Sensors Council and CIS chapters in Hyderabad, chairing the Section, leading INDICON 2024, or chairing the IEEE committees that safeguard the integrity of our technical programs and conferences.

As Chair-Elect, I want that same discipline working for every member in every Section across India.
